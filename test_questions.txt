Semantic | What can someone do in their day-to-day life to anticipate when a migraine attack might hit? | Track sleep, energy and stress. In a 477-person app study, poor sleep (overall or the night before) or low energy the day before predicted morning attacks, while more stress or unusually high energy predicted afternoon and evening attacks.
Semantic | How can people living with migraine cope with feeling judged or dismissed by others? | Connect with others who live with migraine. Almost half of people with chronic migraine feel stigma, and stigma may hurt quality of life more than the headache itself.
Keyword | What is eptinezumab-jjmr, how is it given, and how often? | It is VYEPTI, a prescription medicine for the preventive treatment of migraine in adults, given by a healthcare provider as an intravenous infusion every 3 months.
Keyword | Which monoclonal antibodies that block CGRP are mentioned, and what are the options if you relapse on a CGRP antibody drug? | Atogepant, eptinezumab, erenumab, fremanezumab and galcanezumab. After a relapse you can switch to a different CGRP medication or try botulinum toxin injection.
Multi-Chunk | What classes of preventive medication can a doctor prescribe for chronic migraine, and which nondrug procedures might also help? | Blood pressure medicines (atenolol, propranolol, verapamil), anti-seizure medicines (Depakote, sodium valproate), tricyclic antidepressants (amitriptyline), botulinum toxin injections and CGRP monoclonal antibodies. Nondrug options are nerve stimulation, biofeedback, transcranial magnetic stimulation and acupuncture.
